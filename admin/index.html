Overview

Everything an admin can reach, in one place.

…
Everything here is enforced by the database, not by this page. These screens decide what to show you. Row Level Security decides what anyone is allowed to do — including you, and including anyone who opens this page with the guard deleted.

Users and roles

Who has an account, and what each of them may do. Role changes are confirmed by name before they are saved.

Content

Diseases, articles and hospitals. Drafts stay invisible to the public until they are published.

Emergency contacts

Ambulance, fire, police, poison control. A wrong number here is the worst thing this site can print, so each one is typed twice.

Reports

What readers have flagged as wrong. Resolving or dismissing one needs a note saying why.

Your profile

Your display name, your language, and your password.

What is not here

No traffic charts, no visitor counts, no activity feed. This site does not record what anyone searched for or which disease page they opened — that is identifiable health data, and the way to keep it private is not to collect it. There is nothing to chart, and that is the intended state rather than a missing feature.
