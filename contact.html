Contact Us

Two ways to reach the MedCare team: send an email, or call. There is no contact form on this page, and nothing about you is collected here.

This is not an emergency line

Nobody watches this inbox or this phone around the clock, and no one can be diagnosed or treated by email. In a medical emergency, call 192 for an ambulance.

Tap a number to call it, or the address to open your mail app.

What we can help with

Anything about the website: a page that looks wrong, a hospital or pharmacy whose details have changed, a broken link, or a problem using the site with a screen reader or on a slow connection.

We do not give medical advice by email or on the phone, and we cannot read test results, book appointments, or say which medicine to take. For that you need a doctor — the hospital list and the pharmacy list are there to help you find one.

Something wrong on a page

Every disease and article page carries a Report inaccuracy link at the bottom. That reaches the editors with the page already attached, which is faster and more exact than an email describing it. Email is the right choice for everything else.

Privacy

Writing to us is not anonymous: your email address, or your phone number, arrives with your message, and the reply goes back to it. Nothing else about you is stored, and what you send is not added to any profile or passed to anybody outside the team. The privacy policy covers the rest.
