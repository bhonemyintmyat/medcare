Contact details

The address and the numbers on the Contact us page.

What a reader sees

These are the only two ways to reach the team that contact.html offers: one email address and up to four phone numbers. There is no contact form on that page and nothing is collected from a reader there, so what is typed below is the whole of it.

The mailbox a reader's message will arrive at. This screen can only check that it looks like an address; whether mail sent there reaches a person is something no form can know, so send a test message to it and wait for a reply before trusting a change. A contact page pointing at an inbox nobody opens is worse than one with no address at all.

Up to 4.
With no address and no number saved, the Contact us page keeps the details written into the file it was published with. Nobody lands on a blank page — but the details a reader is given are then whatever was last deployed, not what is on this screen.

What a reader will see

How this differs from everything else here

  • There is no draft, and no review Every other screen in this area writes a row that waits at pending until an admin publishes it. This one has no such step: Save puts the address and the numbers in front of readers immediately. Read the preview before pressing it.
  • These are not the emergency numbers 192, 191, 199 and the helplines are their own screen, and they are what somebody in trouble is looking for. Nothing typed here reaches that page, and an office line does not belong on it.
  • It is public the moment it is saved site_settings is world-readable, including to visitors who are not signed in — that is how the Contact us page reads it. Never put a personal mobile here that should not be on a public page.
  • It is the only setting this area may touch Maintenance mode, the site-wide notice and the legal pages live in the same table and stay admin-only. The database policy behind this screen names one key and refuses the rest — supabase_contact_editors.sql.
